# Megam.io

Megam was an open-source cloud management and platform system built from
Chennai for operators running private and hybrid infrastructure. Rio/OS was the
second phase: a private-cloud operating-system direction that followed the
earlier Megam platform work. The site tells that history in plain terms so a
first-time visitor can understand what was built and why it changed.
